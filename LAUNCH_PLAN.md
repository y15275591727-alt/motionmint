# MotionMint launch plan

## Launch positioning

Lead with one visual promise:

> Upload one photo and one motion clip. Get a share-ready video in minutes.

Avoid launching with a long list of model names. Model names build trust with developers, not creators. Keep Viggle-powered technology in the FAQ and API/compliance pages.

## First 30 days

### Week 1

- Publish the landing page, free studio, three trial renders, and referral link.
- Add analytics for studio entry, first generated clip, download, and upgrade click.
- Recruit 10 creators to produce before-and-after clips.

### Week 2

- Publish 12 short-form demos from those creator clips.
- Create separate landing pages for:
  - character swap
  - photo to motion
  - AI music-video character
  - UGC ad production
- Start founder-led outreach in creator, cosplay, dance, and short-video communities.

### Week 3

- Launch the referral reward after the first verified generated clip.
- Run a "main character challenge" with a weekly featured gallery.
- Test two offers: 100-credit pack versus $9 starter subscription.

### Week 4

- Double down on the best-performing workflow and creative angle.
- Add team plans only after individual paid conversion is measurable.
- Move from raw views to activation and paid conversion as the primary health metrics.

## Acquisition loops

- **Share loop:** every result can produce a branded share card; free results carry a subtle watermark.
- **Referral loop:** reward only after the invited user creates once.
- **Gallery loop:** public showcase is browsable and each item exposes a "remix this motion" entry.
- **Creator loop:** affiliate revenue share for creators who drive paid subscriptions.
- **SEO loop:** landing pages around concrete outcomes instead of generic "AI video".

## Publishing boundary

We can build the website, launch assets, creator briefs, SEO pages, advertising copy, and tracking setup inside this project. Publishing to an external ad account or social account requires the account owner to grant access and approve the campaign.
